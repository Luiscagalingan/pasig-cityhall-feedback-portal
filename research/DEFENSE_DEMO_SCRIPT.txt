DEFENSE LIVE DEMO — RECOMMENDED 8–10 MINUTES
============================================

Before starting:
[ ] Run tests\run_smoke_test.bat
[ ] Prepare Admin, Office Head, and Office Staff accounts
[ ] Prepare one unused valid survey response
[ ] Keep screenshots/video ready in case local services fail

1. PUBLIC PORTAL (1 minute)
- Show office selection, privacy policy, light/dark mode, and mobile layout.
- Show that age 17 is blocked and only 18+ is accepted.

2. FEEDBACK PROCESSING (1 minute)
- Submit valid ratings and Filipino/English/Taglish comment.
- Explain SVM label, confidence indicator, source, and weighted score.
- Repeat the same data to show exact-duplicate protection.

3. OFFICE WORKSPACE (2 minutes)
- Sign in as Office Staff and show assigned-office-only feedback.
- Update an action and request completion.
- Explain why Staff cannot approve final completion.

4. OFFICE HEAD (2 minutes)
- Approve the pending action.
- Show sentiment-review queue and original-label preservation.
- Show CSV preview/rejected rows without importing unreviewed data.

5. ADMINISTRATOR (2 minutes)
- Show consolidated dashboard, office management, archived accounts, and audit log.
- Show encrypted backup modal and retention policy without exposing a passphrase.

6. RESEARCH EVIDENCE (1–2 minutes)
- Show final dataset counts, Cohen's Kappa, cross-validation result,
  independent test metrics, confusion matrix, and error analysis.
- End with limitations and human-oversight statement.

Do not claim:
- Demo samples are actual respondents
- Operational confidence is a calibrated probability
- Fallback output is SVM output
- Institutional approvals exist unless documented
