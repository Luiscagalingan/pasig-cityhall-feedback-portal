CORE PANEL QUESTIONS AND SHORT ANSWERS
======================================

1. Why Linear SVM?
Linear SVM is efficient for sparse high-dimensional TF-IDF text and works well
with limited labeled data, word n-grams, and character n-grams.

2. Where did the data come from?
State the actual approved source. Explain de-identification, sample count,
class distribution, and exclusion rules. Never describe demo data as respondents.

3. Who labeled the comments?
State the two actual reviewers, written labeling guide, Cohen's Kappa result,
and how disagreements were resolved.

4. Is confidence a probability?
No. It is a softmax transformation of LinearSVC decision margins used only for
operational review prioritization. Low-confidence results receive human review.

5. What happens when Python/SVM fails?
The marked fallback supports continuity. Its source is stored and every fallback
result enters human review; it is never represented as SVM research output.

6. How are offices isolated?
Authentication assigns an office_id. Office routes check roles and every
record query/action includes that office_id. Automated security tests verify it.

7. How is spam controlled?
The portal blocks exact duplicates, applies a 60-second cooldown, and limits
successful submissions to three per hashed network/browser identity per hour.

8. Why 18+?
The public survey is adult-only to maintain a clear consent scope. Server and
browser validation reject respondents aged 17 or below.

9. How is privacy protected?
Data minimization instructions, affirmative consent, role/office scoping,
prepared statements, CSRF, audit logs, retention removal, protected folders,
and encrypted backups are implemented.

10. What are the limitations?
Model quality depends on approved representative labels. Sarcasm, complex
negation, and mixed sentiment remain difficult. Human review is retained and
the model supports rather than replaces human judgment.
