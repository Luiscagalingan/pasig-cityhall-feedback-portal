SENTIMENT LABELING GUIDE
========================

Allowed labels: positive, neutral, negative

POSITIVE
- Clear satisfaction, praise, gratitude, or approval of the service.
- Example: "Mabilis at magalang ang staff. Salamat."

NEGATIVE
- Clear complaint, dissatisfaction, harmful delay, discourtesy, error, or unresolved problem.
- Example: "Sobrang tagal at walang malinaw na instruction."

NEUTRAL
- Factual statement without clear emotion, unclear sentiment, or genuinely balanced mixed experience.
- Example: "Nakuha ko ang dokumento pagkatapos ng normal na proseso."

MIXED COMMENTS
- Label the dominant overall evaluation.
- If praise and complaint are equally strong and neither dominates, use neutral.
- Do not label based on a single word without reading the whole comment.

NEGATION
- "Hindi mabilis" is negative, while "hindi naman mabagal" may be positive or neutral depending on context.

REVIEW RULES
1. Reviewers label independently.
2. Reviewers must not see each other's first label.
3. Do not include personal identifiers in the labeling file.
4. Disagreements must be resolved only after Cohen's Kappa is calculated.
5. Record the final label and a short resolution note.
