# Data Privacy and Retention Draft

The system collects sex, age, client classification, service received, four ratings, and a required written comment for service-quality monitoring. Public respondents are instructed not to enter names, phone numbers, diagnoses, or unnecessary identifiers.

Access is limited by role and office scope. Archived accounts and archived offices are blocked. Audit logs record protected activity. Feedback corrections use void/restore controls so the original record and reason remain traceable.

Before production deployment, Pasig City CHD must approve:

- the official privacy contact and complaint channel;
- the legal basis and final privacy-notice wording;
- retention periods for feedback, audit logs, imports, notifications, and backups;
- deletion or anonymization procedures after retention expires;
- who may approve training examples and export research datasets;
- backup storage, encryption, access, and restoration procedures.
