SVM TAGALOG/TAGLISH UPDATE
===========================

Updated demo dataset:
- 300 total labeled comments
- 100 positive
- 100 neutral
- 100 negative

Added support for common shorthand/slang and informal spellings, including:
nmn, dba, db, di, ndi, hndi, kc, kse, lng, kht, aq, q, sna,
tnx, thx, ty, tyvm, pls, plz, oks, goods, solid, angas, ambait,
ambilis, ambagal, waley, badtrip, dedma, keri, meh, mid, hassle,
bagaaal, tagaaal, and related complete-sentence examples.

Files:
- ml/data/sample_training.csv
- ml/data/tagalog_slang_dictionary.csv
- ml/normalize_text.py
- ml/SLANG_AND_SHORTCUTS.md
- ml/models/model_metrics.json

Demo holdout result after retraining:
- Accuracy: 81.67%
- Macro F1: 81.58%

IMPORTANT: These are development/demo results. Final research metrics must use
an approved, de-identified, manually labeled CHD dataset and a documented,
separate evaluation set.
